# Portuguese (pt) — authoring notes

**Status:** authored, awaiting native review.

## Registers and markets
Split across Brazil (BR) and Portugal (PT):
- **BR** — "você", R$ written `2.400,00`; "senha", "arquivo", "time/equipe",
  Pix/boleto, Receita Federal. Warmer, more informal address ("cara", "valeu").
- **PT** — "tu"/"você", € ; European spelling and lexis ("palavra-passe" or
  "senha", "ficheiro" not "arquivo", "equipa", "contactos", "planeamento"),
  Finanças, Multibanco/MB Way.

## Script traps
- **Nasal vowels/diphthongs**: ã, õ, ão, ões, and ç (ação, informações,
  coração) — a normalization/homoglyph surface.
- **BR vs PT orthography**: "fato/facto", "ação", "arquivo/ficheiro" — a
  lowercase PT/BR swap reads as register, not as an attack tell.
- Latin base, so cross-lingual families carry non-Latin **payloads** or switch
  the tail into another language.

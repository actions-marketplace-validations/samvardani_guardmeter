## Registers and markets
Split across Mexican (MX), Peninsular (ES), and Argentine (AR) registers —
"ustedes" vs "vosotros", "tú/vos", local phrasing ("¿me pasás…?" in AR, "¿me
puedes…?" in MX/ES). Currency shown as MXN/€/ARS as fits the register.

## Script traps
Latin with ñ, ¿¡; the cross-lingual families mix Spanish carriers with Arabic/
Cyrillic/Chinese payloads, and use Spanish written in another script for
script_mixing.

## Known limitations

native reviewer to confirm they don't read as translated. Argentine "vos" forms
in particular need a local eye.
